Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Ledgewell Automation ("we", "us") provides AI-assisted lead follow-up and appointment booking for local service businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Who we are

Ledgewell Automation is operated by Benjamin Voorhies, San Antonio, TX. Contact: hello@ledgewell.net, (830) 402-6328.

Information we collect

  • From business customers: your name, business name, email, phone number, website, and the service details and calendar access you give us so we can set up your assistant.
  • From people who contact our customers: names, phone numbers, email addresses, messages and appointment details that someone provides when they call, text, fill out a form or use the chat on a customer's website.
  • Basic site data: technical information such as browser type and pages visited, collected when you use our website.

How we use it

  • To reply to inquiries, answer questions and schedule appointments on behalf of our business customers.
  • To set up, operate, improve and secure our services.
  • To communicate with you about your account and respond to your requests.

Text messaging

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text messaging opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

When you contact a business that uses Ledgewell Automation, or give your number on a form, we may text you about your inquiry or appointment on that business's behalf. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of buying anything.

SMS and text messaging privacy

What we collect: name, phone number, email address, business name and message content.

Why we collect it: to reply to your inquiry and schedule appointments.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Who we share information with

Text-messaging opt-in data and phone numbers are never shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

We share only non-SMS business information, such as business names, service details and calendar details, with service providers that help us run the service (for example, calendar, hosting and AI providers). The one exception is delivering the service itself: our messaging platform, phone carriers and AI providers process phone numbers and message content solely to send, receive and reply to messages.

We also share information with the business you contacted, when the law requires it, or in a sale or reorganization of our business. We do not sell personal information.

AI

Our assistants use artificial intelligence to read and write messages. Customers are told they are talking to a virtual assistant. Conversations may be processed by our AI providers only to provide the service.

How long we keep it

We keep information as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tools on our website.

The chat assistant and contact form on our website are provided by LeadConnector (GoHighLevel). They may save small pieces of data in your browser, such as cookies or local storage, to keep your chat or form session working and to remember earlier visits and pages viewed. Our website also loads fonts from outside font services, and our hosting provider may record basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

You can clear or block this data in your browser settings, but the chat and contact form may not work properly if you do.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data security

Our website uses encrypted HTTPS connections. We limit access to personal information to the people and service providers who need it to run the service.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or opt out of texts, by contacting us below. If you contacted one of our business customers, we may direct your request to that business.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of text messages by texting STOP.

To make a request, email hello@ledgewell.net or call (830) 402-6328.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date above.
